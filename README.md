
# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, salary distribution, and departmental performance. The dashboard helps organizations understand workforce trends, identify attrition patterns, and support data-driven HR decision-making.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard focuses on the following primary KPIs:

1. **Total Employees:** 1,417
2. **Active Employees:** 1,186
3. **Attrition Count:** 231
4. **Attrition Rate:** 16.3%
5. **Average Age:** 36.94 Years
6. **Average Experience:** 7.04 Years

## Features

- Employee Attrition Analysis by Department
- Attrition Analysis by Salary Slab
- Job Role and Job Satisfaction Analysis
- Gender-wise Attrition Distribution
- Age Group Distribution Analysis
- Experience-based Attrition Trends
- Department-wise Employee Count
- Interactive Age Group Filters

## Insights and Conclusions

1. The organization recorded an attrition rate of **16.3%** with 231 employees leaving the company.
2. The **26–35 age group** represents the largest portion of the workforce.
3. Employees in lower salary slabs experienced higher attrition.
4. Attrition is higher among employees with fewer years of experience.
5. Male employees account for a larger share of total attrition.
6. Job satisfaction levels vary across different job roles.
7. The dashboard helps identify key factors affecting employee retention.

## Recommendations

- Improve employee retention in departments with high attrition.
- Enhance employee engagement and job satisfaction programs.
- Provide learning and career growth opportunities.
- Review salary structures and employee benefits.
- Strengthen onboarding and retention strategies for new employees.
- Monitor attrition trends regularly using HR analytics.
- Use data-driven insights to support HR decision-making.
